# Glossary

## Coordinated Intelligence

The use of multiple systems, models, tools, or workflows in a shared structure
that supports human intent.

## Isolated AI

An AI interaction pattern where a single system responds without broader
coordination, durable context, or tool-aware workflow structure.

## Human-Centered Collaboration

A model where AI supports human creativity and judgment instead of replacing
them.

## Orchestration

The coordination of specialized systems toward a shared goal.

## Creative Workflow

The movement from idea to exploration, drafting, review, execution, and
iteration.
