# Research Areas

Current public research areas include:

- human-AI collaboration
- creative workflows
- AI-assisted development
- multi-system cooperation
- workflow experimentation
- knowledge organization
- future interaction models

## Human-AI Collaboration

How can AI support human intent without displacing human authorship?

## Creative Workflows

How can tools reduce friction between idea, draft, review, and execution?

## AI-Assisted Development

How can AI systems help with planning, implementation, testing, documentation,
and release work while keeping humans in control?

## Multi-System Cooperation

How should multiple specialized tools or models coordinate without blurring
responsibility?

## Knowledge Organization

How can working memory, decisions, research notes, and project context remain
clear over long-running creative work?

## Future Interaction Models

What should replace isolated chat windows when collaboration becomes more
structured and tool-aware?
